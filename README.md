Virtual Pet Simulator

A browser-based virtual pet game where you create, care for, and manage your own pet while staying within a budget.

Features

Create your own pet (Dog, Cat, Bunny, Lizard, Parrot, Horse)

Track pet stats:
Hunger
Happiness
Energy
Health
Hygiene

Money management system

Expense tracking (food, vet, toys, supplies)

Interactive actions:
Feed
Play
Clean
Visit the vet

Trivia mini-game to earn money

Dynamic mood system based on pet stats

Pop-up alerts when money is insufficient

Technologies Used

HTML
CSS
JavaScript

How to Run
Download or clone the project files.

Make sure all files are in the same folder:
index.html
script.js
style.css
images/ (folder with pet images)

Open index.html in your browser.

How to Play
Enter your pet’s name.
Choose a pet type.
Click "Create Pet".
Use action buttons to take care of your pet:
Feed - reduces hunger (costs $5)
Play - increases happiness (costs $3)
Clean - improves hygiene (costs $2)
Vet - improves health (costs $20)

Click "Earn Money" to answer trivia questions.

Monitor:
Your pet’s stats
Your remaining money
Your total spending

Game Mechanics
Stats range from 0 to 100
Actions cost money
If you run out of money, actions are blocked

Trivia rewards:
Correct answer: +$10
Wrong answer: -10 health

Mood system:
Sick - low health
Sad - low happiness
Energetic - high energy
Happy - default state
